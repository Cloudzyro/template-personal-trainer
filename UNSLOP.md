# Unslop pass

Done with the unslop skill (by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0), 25 November 2026. The template was designed against the signs list from the first line, then audited after the build.

## Audit

Slop level: light. Signs found in the first build and fixed:

- Package buttons wrapped onto two lines ("Apply for starter pack") on the programs page, so three cards had uneven buttons
- Dead band on the apply page: page header padding plus section padding stacked to about 160px before the form
- Optional hints under field labels dropped onto their own line, breaking the label rhythm
- Before and after tables on the results page had a different column width per story, so the numbers didn't line up down the page
- A British word in sample copy ("rota")
- An arrow-like "assess > plan > review" notation

Checked and clean: no uppercase tracked labels, no pill badges, no gradients, glows or blobs, headings at 500, no italic, no exclamation marks, no trailing periods on headings or buttons, no hover scale or looping motion, one accent.

## Direction

- Product: the site of a one-person strength coach, so people can see programs and prices and apply for coaching
- Character: direct, methodical, encouraging
- World: a training log on a clipboard, chalk, a loaded barbell, a weight room under plain light
- Moments: the training log card in the hero with a week of lifts, and the before and after figures in the results
- Signature: set and rep notation in mono ("4 x 6 @ RPE 7"), used for lifts, programs and approach details only

## What changed

- Spacing: tighter top padding when a page header is followed by a section; programs groups at 48 to 80px
- Copy: package buttons say "Apply" with a full accessible label; "rota" became "schedule"; no arrow notation
- Type: optional hints sit inline with their label
- Details: results tables use fixed columns so before and after figures align across stories
- Visuals and motion: unchanged; one press scale on buttons, color changes on hover
